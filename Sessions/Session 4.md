# Situation 2: The Thermal Wastes
Potential to visit a stray Data Well, rumored to contain one of the [[Faint Divinities]]. It only has a power line connected to it, no data access. Using the network tether here confirms this, as it just sounds like many voices whispering gibberish over each other.

End of section: Vast ravine with a chilly breeze that runs through it. There is no sign of the Thermal Oasis on the other side

# Situation 3: The Cliff Dwellers
There is a way down from the top of the ravine, a la a Via Ferrata, though the safety cable has long disappeared.

Environmental notes:
1. Rushing river at the bottom of the ravine
2. The walls are coated in shadow from the height, unless it is around noon
3. The rock is cool to the touch
4. The winds get stronger toward the bottom, bringing the smell of slate

## The Abandoned Dwellings
A multi-tiered former city, the only surface dwelling in the thermal wastes.
Large metal retaining wall, now burst through. There is a large tunnel (roughly 30 ft across) in a perfect circle carved directly into the mountain face, through many of the ruined buildings.
