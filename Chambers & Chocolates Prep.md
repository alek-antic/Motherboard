# Critterfolk transformations

## Arahnii (Spiderfolk)
Arahnii gain the following experience:
- *Adept Climber* +4
**Traits:**
1. *Web Slinger.* Mark a Stress to make one enemy within Very Close range *Restrained* and *Vulnerable*.
2. *Fear of Fire.* If you mark an HP from any source of fire, also mark a Stress.

## Avvin (Birdfolk)
Avvin gain the following experience:
- *Fledgling Wings* +4
**Traits:**
1. *Eagle-eyed.* Spend a Hope to gain Advantage on a Perceive or Sense roll.
2. *Aerial Ace.* When in the air, mark a Stress to gain Advantage on any roll.
3. *Brittle Bones.* If you mark HP from any source of blunt damage, mark one more.

## Cannis (Dogfolk)
Cannis gain the following experience:
- *Keen Sense of Smell* +4
**Traits:**
1. *Fleet of Foot.* You may move into Far range without making an Agility roll.
2. *Loyal to a Fault.* When an ally within Very Close range is hit by an attack, you may intercept the damage. If you do not, you must mark one Stress.

## Chippin (Squirrelfolk)
Chippin gain the following experience:
- *Acrobat* +4
**Traits:**
1. *Well Fed.* Gain +1 HP slot.
2. *Timid.* If you would mark Stress from any source, mark two instead.
