# Summary
From the outside, the Beehive appears to be a production facility, spewing out many drone-like remnants every day. However, there is no concrete evidence that the drones are actually manufactured here.

No one has ever entered to investigate this sprawling underground complex and returned to tell the tale.

The area surrounding the beehive has next to no cover because it used to be an airfield.

[[Carrowcroft Walkaway]] hovers nearby in case of people in danger of the bees
