# Summary
Underground mine to bring up copper needed to maintain and repair the data wells and the spider-like network above the [[Echo Vale]].

The mine itself is run using a combination of mechanicals and tame remnants.

## Physical Layout
The mine runs through the western mountains, spanning 30 levels. The town is in a large cavern, platform city (Blightown from DS1)

The mines themselves are mostly unremarkable. Mine carts rush by, clanking along the tracks. Elevators churn steadily, raising and lowering both miners and carts, usually simultaneously.

## Data Well
There is a data well at the top of the mountain above the trove. Connections to it have been made through the earth in order to help operations in the mine. This is one of the reasons it requires so much maintenance.

## Post Remnant’s Fury
Lots of the mine’s operation has slowed down dramatically
